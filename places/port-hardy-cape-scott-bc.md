# Port Hardy + Cape Scott, BC

![San Josef Bay, Cape Scott](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Long%20White%20Beach%2C%20Cape%20Scott%20Provincial%20Park.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because Vancouver → Port Hardy is currently about **1h10 nonstop**, and Port Hardy is the practical gateway to the northwestern tip of Vancouver Island: Cape Scott, the North Coast Trail, enormous beaches, old-growth rainforest and the kind of coast that looks far more remote than the flight time suggests.

## the wait-what moment

Checked **2026-09-04**:

- YVR → YZT currently runs nonstop on Pacific Coastal.
- Current schedule data shows roughly **9 flights/week**.
- Flight time is about **1h10**.
- Cape Scott Park begins roughly **64 km west of Port Hardy** by a mix of public and active logging roads.

BC Parks describes Cape Scott as more than **115 km of ocean frontage**, including roughly **30 km of remote beaches**. Nels Bight alone stretches over 2.4 km.

That is an absurd amount of wilderness to unlock with a one-hour regional flight.

## what it seems like

This is the northern-Vancouver-Island version of the repo's favourite trick: let the airplane delete the tedious half of the map, then spend the saved time somewhere wild.

Port Hardy itself is the staging point. Cape Scott is the payoff: white sand, sea stacks, bogs, old-growth Sitka spruce, wolves, bears, surf and trails that can become as serious as we want them to.

## things worth doing

- For the easy version, drive / shuttle to **San Josef Bay** and make the beach the destination.
- For the committed version, hike to **Nels Bight** or build a proper Cape Scott backpacking trip.
- For the genuinely ambitious version, investigate the **North Coast Trail** and water-taxi logistics from Port Hardy.
- Spend enough time around Port Hardy to make the north-Island culture and fishing-town context part of the trip.
- Keep an eye on ferries and marine trips; Port Hardy also sits on the coastal transport web north toward Prince Rupert.

## from Vancouver

This is almost offensively efficient.

**YVR → tiny plane → Port Hardy in ~70 minutes → beach / rainforest country.**

Driving from Vancouver would consume most of a day plus a ferry. The flight turns the same region into a short-trip proposition.

## rough trip idea

**3 nights, easy version:**

1. Fly YVR → YZT; Port Hardy evening.
2. Shuttle / drive to San Josef Bay; long beach day.
3. Another north-Island coast / wildlife day.
4. Fly home.

**4–6 nights:** turn Cape Scott into the main event with overnight hiking.
