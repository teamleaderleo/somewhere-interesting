# Zhangjiajie, Hunan, China

![Zhangjiajie National Forest Park](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Zhangjiajie%20National%20Forest%20Park.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because **Nantong → Zhangjiajie is about 2h10 nonstop**.

Two hours from the Yangtze Delta and suddenly the earth has turned into a forest of stone towers.

## the landscape

UNESCO describes Wulingyuan as more than **26,000 hectares** dominated by **3,000+ narrow quartz-sandstone pillars and peaks**, many over 200 m high, mixed with ravines, streams, pools, waterfalls, caves and natural bridges.

This is one of those places where the photos look like somebody overdid the concept art and then geology went along with it.

## things I would actually want to do

- Spend a full day inside Zhangjiajie National Forest Park with no attempt to “complete” it.
- Go up Tianzi Mountain for the pillar panoramas.
- Walk around when cloud and mist are hanging between the peaks.
- Do Tianmen Mountain separately.
- Stay long enough to see the place early or late in the day, outside the thickest tour-group hours.

## the Nantong portal

Checked **2026-08-29**:

- **NTG → DYG** nonstop: about **2h10**.
- Current schedule shows several nonstop flights per week.

The existence of this route makes Zhangjiajie feel less like a major expedition and more like an aggressive long weekend.

## rough trip

**Day 1:** fly in, settle, light exploration.

**Day 2:** Wulingyuan / National Forest Park all day.

**Day 3:** another park sector or Tianzi Mountain.

**Day 4:** Tianmen Mountain, then home.
