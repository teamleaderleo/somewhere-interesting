# Kunming, Yunnan, China

![Stone Forest near Kunming](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Stone%20Forest%20near%20Kunming.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because **Nantong → Kunming is about 2h50 nonstop**, with current schedules showing roughly two flights a day.

Kunming itself is appealing; its greater power is that it is a **launchpad into Yunnan**.

## the immediate hook

The nearby **Stone Forest / Shilin** is exactly what the name promises: towers and blades of limestone that read like a petrified forest.

Kunming also sits high enough to have a milder climate than a lot of southern China, and from the city you can keep going deeper into Yunnan toward Dali, Lijiang, Shangri-La, Xishuangbanna and a dozen other future repo problems.

## things I would actually want to do

- Spend a day in Shilin Stone Forest.
- Walk around Dianchi / the lake edge.
- Eat Yunnan mushrooms when the season is right and somebody competent is cooking them.
- Treat Kunming as the first night of a longer Yunnan trip.
- Build a future Dali/Lijiang rail itinerary from here.

## the Nantong portal

## rough trip

For Kunming alone, 3 days works.

For the trip I actually want: **fly Nantong → Kunming, then keep going through Yunnan by train** and let the trip become something larger.
