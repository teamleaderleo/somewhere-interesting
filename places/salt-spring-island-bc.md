# Salt Spring Island, British Columbia

![Ganges Harbour, Salt Spring Island](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Harbor%20in%20Ganges%2C%20Saltspring%20Island%2C%20British%20Columbia%2C%20Canada%2008.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because Vancouver can turn into **Ganges Harbour by seaplane**.

Salt Spring is one of those destinations where the transport is half the romance: downtown Vancouver or YVR South → little plane over the Salish Sea → land on the water in the island's main village.

## what got me

The island's official tourism site describes it as quirky, creative and farm-driven: artisan studios, farmers, local food, spas, wineries and a general permission slip to slow down.

The Saturday Market in Ganges runs in season under the gloriously strict principle: **Make it, Bake it, Grow it.**

## from Vancouver

Current Harbour Air material lists scheduled service linking greater Vancouver with Ganges Harbour, and the airline's 2026 network includes Salt Spring from YVR South.

Historically this is roughly a **35-minute seaplane hop** from Vancouver Harbour; always check the live schedule because frequencies change by season.

## what I would actually do

- Fly in by seaplane at least one direction.
- Time the trip for the Saturday Market if convenient.
- Wander Ganges slowly.
- Hit farm stands / studios.
- Hike somewhere quiet, then eat whatever the island grew.
- Stay overnight because this place's entire point is that rushing looks stupid here.
