## why this is here

Because Frankfurt apparently has a **three-hour portal to a Mediterranean island in Tunisia**.

Djerba is one of those places whose ingredients sound like someone kept adding things after the pitch was already good enough: long pale beaches, warm Mediterranean water, palm trees, whitewashed villages, pottery workshops, old souks, seafood, a historic Jewish community, forts, mosques, and big resort strips all sharing one island.

This is less “fly to North Africa and begin an expedition” and more “get on a plane after breakfast and be on a Tunisian island before lunch.”

## the thing that got me

Djerba seems to offer **two holidays at once**.

There is the easy version: Sidi Mahrez beach, resort, pool, swim, eat, sleep.

Then there is the island behind it: **Houmt Souk**, the main town, with covered markets, a fishing harbour, old merchant inns, cafés and Borj El Kebir fort; **Guellala**, known for pottery; and **El Ghriba**, a historic working synagogue and pilgrimage site.

So you could absolutely spend a day being horizontal by the sea and then wake up the next morning and go wander around a functioning Tunisian market town.

## things I would actually want to do

- Stay near the beach, but escape the resort strip repeatedly.
- Go to **Houmt Souk** early for the market and harbour.
- Eat grilled fish somewhere with no attempt at international-airport pricing.
- Visit Guellala pottery workshops.
- Visit El Ghriba respectfully.
- Drive around the smaller villages instead of treating the island as one giant hotel.
- Spend one evening doing almost nothing except watching the light change over the Mediterranean.

## the Frankfurt portal

Checked **2026-08-29**:

- FRA → DJE has nonstop service from several airlines.
- Typical nonstop time is about **2h45–2h55**.
- Lufthansa/Discover was advertising September round trips from about **€196** on some dates.
- Same-day pricing was much higher, which is another reminder that this repo rewards people who plan before standing at the gate yelling “TAXI.”

This is one of the highest **WTF-to-flight-time ratios** we have found so far.

## rough trip idea

### four days

**Day 1:** land, beach, eat, become Mediterranean.

**Day 2:** Houmt Souk morning, fort, market, seafood, lazy afternoon.

**Day 3:** Guellala + El Ghriba + wandering around the island.

**Day 4:** beach until airport.
