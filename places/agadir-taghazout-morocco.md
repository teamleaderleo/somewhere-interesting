## why this is here

Because Frankfurt apparently has a **€100-ish direct portal to Morocco's Atlantic coast**.

Condor was advertising Frankfurt (FRA) → Agadir (AGA) from **€107.99** when checked on **2026-08-29**, with September examples as low as €112.99. Condor says it serves the route around four times a week across its current schedule.

And the destination at the other end is not merely “a Moroccan city with a beach.” It is a whole Atlantic / surf / souk / mountain situation.

## the immediate reaction

Agadir has a roughly **10 km seafront**, a broad bay, cafés and restaurants facing the Atlantic, and **Souk El Had**, which Morocco's tourism office describes as having more than 6,000 shops.

Then go a little north and you hit **Taghazout**: former fishing village, now one of Morocco's famous surf zones. The Moroccan tourism office specifically calls out **Anchor Point** and **Killer Point**, with waves for beginners through experienced surfers and a beach backed by the High Atlas / argan country.

So you can plausibly spend one day loafing beside a calm bay and the next getting thrown around by Atlantic swell.

## things I would actually want to do

- Spend a slow morning on Agadir's waterfront.
- Get lost in Souk El Had and buy something I did not plan to buy.
- Eat tagine / grilled fish / whatever smells best.
- Go to Taghazout and attempt surfing with appropriate humility.
- Watch competent surfers at Anchor Point.
- Do a day inland toward the Anti-Atlas / argan landscape.
- Stay somewhere where sunset involves the Atlantic.

## the Frankfurt portal

Checked **2026-08-29**:

- Condor: **FRA → AGA** direct.
- Advertised future economy fare: **from €107.99**.
- Sample September fares on the page ranged from about **€112.99 to €252.99** depending on date.
- Condor says the route runs around **4 times weekly** in the current schedule.

That is firmly in the category of **“this costs less than I emotionally expect crossing continents to cost.”**

## rough trip idea

### four days

**Day 1:** Agadir waterfront, souk, enormous dinner.

**Day 2:** Taghazout, surf lesson, beach, sunset.

**Day 3:** inland day — mountains / argan country / smaller towns.

**Day 4:** deliberately accomplish almost nothing beside the Atlantic.
