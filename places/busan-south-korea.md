# Busan, South Korea

## why this is here

Because Shanghai → Busan is currently one of those international routes that barely feels international in flight-time terms.

Checked **2026-09-04**:

- PVG → PUS currently has about **34 nonstop flights/week**.
- Five airlines are in the market: China Eastern, Jeju Air, Korean Air, Shanghai Airlines and Spring Airlines.
- Fastest scheduled nonstop is around **1h35**; many flights run closer to two hours.

That is an absurdly easy portal to a Korean coastal city with seafood markets, beaches, mountains, temples and a very different mood from Seoul.

## the wait-what moment

The route frequency is the revelation.

This is not “find the one good flight.” There are roughly five nonstops a day in the current schedule. Busan can genuinely operate as a spontaneous long weekend from Shanghai.

Then the city itself sprawls along mountains and sea, which means a weekend can move between Jagalchi's seafood tanks, old hillside neighborhoods, beaches, coastal walks and temples without ever feeling like a generic city break.

## what it seems like

A big city that keeps falling into the ocean.

Busan works best when the trip follows the coast: **Jagalchi / Nampo**, then a beach district, then cliffs or temple, then another seafood meal because apparently that is the law here.

It is denser and more urban than a pure beach trip, while still giving enough sea and topography to feel like a proper escape.

## things worth doing

- Eat through **Jagalchi Market** and the surrounding Nampo area.
- Walk a real piece of the coast: Taejongdae, Igidae or another route depending where we stay.
- See **Haedong Yonggungsa** for the unusual seaside-temple setting.
- Use Haeundae or Gwangalli as an evening-by-the-water stop instead of an all-day beach obligation.
- Explore one older hillside neighborhood such as Gamcheon with enough time to wander beyond the photo spots.
- Eat dwaeji-gukbap, milmyeon and seafood; this trip has excellent food density.

## from Shanghai

This is perhaps the cleanest “international weekend with almost domestic-flight friction” entry in the repo.

**Shanghai → Busan: as little as about 1h35 nonstop, with roughly 34 flights a week.**

That should sit in the same mental drawer as Fukuoka and Jeju.

## rough trip idea

**2 nights:** Nampo/Jagalchi + one coastal district + temple or cliff walk.

**3 nights:** enough time to stay slow, eat properly and let the city stretch from one side of the coast to another.
