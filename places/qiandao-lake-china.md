# Qiandao Lake / Thousand Islets Lake, Zhejiang

## why this is here

Because there is a high-speed rail station literally named **Qiandaohu**, and current 2026 schedules put the quickest Shanghai trains in roughly the **sub-two-hour** range.

The lake itself is a huge reservoir scattered with forested islands. This is less "major attraction checklist" and more "rent somewhere with a view, get on a boat, eat fish, disappear for two days."

## from Shanghai

Checked **2026-08-29**:

- Qiandaohu Station has direct high-speed service from Shanghai.
- Current timetable summaries show the quickest trips around **1h48**.
- The station is about **27 km from the lake tourism area**, so there is still a road transfer after the train.

## what I would actually do

- Treat it as a two-night reset instead of a frantic sightseeing trip.
- Boat / kayak depending on season and local rules.
- Find a hill or viewpoint above the water.
- Eat the local fish dishes.
- Pair with Hangzhou or Huangshan only if the trip is long enough to stay slow.
