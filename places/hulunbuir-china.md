# Hulunbuir, Inner Mongolia

![Hulunbuir grassland](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Hulun%20Buir.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because Nantong currently has a nonstop flight to Hailar, and the destination on the other end looks like someone replaced Jiangsu with several hundred kilometres of sky.

Hailar is the practical air gateway to Hulunbuir: immense grasslands, rivers, wetlands, horses, long summer evenings and a completely different climate / visual scale from the lower Yangtze.

## the wait-what moment

Checked **2026-09-04**:

- NTG → HLD currently runs nonstop.
- Current schedule data shows roughly **6 departures a week**.
- Flight time is roughly **3h15–3h35** depending schedule.
- Nantong Airport specifically listed Hailar among its new/popular tourism routes for the 2026 summer-autumn season.

Three-and-a-bit hours from Nantong to the Hulunbuir grasslands is exactly the kind of geographic compression this repo exists for.

## what it seems like

This is a horizon trip.

The attraction is less a checklist of monuments and more the scale of the place: open steppe, braided and meandering rivers, grazing animals, huge cloud systems and long drives where the landscape keeps being the main event.

Hailar itself is the transport base. The interesting trip starts when we drive outward into the grassland.

## things worth doing

- Drive out toward the **Morigele / Mergel River grassland** area and spend time somewhere the horizon is doing most of the work.
- Stay at least one night outside Hailar rather than treating the grassland as a bus excursion.
- Horse riding if the operator and animal-welfare situation looks good.
- Eat the Inner Mongolia version of the trip properly: lamb, dairy, noodles, hotpot, local breakfast.
- Consider pairing Hailar with **Manzhouli** or another border-region stop if the road trip grows beyond 3–4 days.

## from Nantong

This is one of NTG's strongest summer escape routes because the payoff is visual and climatic at the same time.

**Jiangsu humid summer → one flight → northern grassland.**

That sentence is already most of the sales pitch.

## rough trip idea

**3 nights:**

1. Fly NTG → HLD; sleep Hailar or head directly outward if arrival timing works.
2. Full grassland day + overnight outside the city.
3. Longer road loop / river / riding / another grassland stay.
4. Return to Hailar and fly home.

A longer version could add Manzhouli and the border-country feeling of far northeastern Inner Mongolia.
