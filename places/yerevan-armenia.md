# Yerevan, Armenia

## why this is here

Because Yerevan is a **four-hour nonstop flight from Frankfurt** and gives you pink-stone boulevards, cafés, Armenian food, giant public art, brandy, old churches and Mount Ararat hanging on the horizon like somebody painted it into the background.

The official city tourism site calls Yerevan “the youngest of the oldest cities in the world,” which is exactly the sort of sentence that gets a place admitted here.

## the thing that got me

The **Cascade**.

It is part staircase, part monument, part outdoor art space, part city viewpoint. You climb through gardens and sculpture and eventually get the classic Yerevan view toward Ararat.

Then the city keeps going: Republic Square, cafés everywhere, markets, music, late dinners, Armenian wine and brandy, and an urban culture that seems very happy to keep you sitting at a table longer than planned.

And Yerevan is an excellent base for the next layer outward: **Garni**, **Geghard**, Lake Sevan, monasteries, mountains and day trips all radiate from the capital.

## things I would actually want to do

- Climb the Cascade around golden hour.
- Spend an evening around Republic Square and then eat for several consecutive hours.
- Drink Armenian wine and brandy with reckless educational commitment.
- Go to Garni and Geghard for a day.
- Find old neighbourhood streets between the grander boulevards.
- Sit in cafés and make absolutely no attempt to optimize the trip.

## the Frankfurt portal

Checked **2026-08-29**:

- FRA → EVN has nonstop service from **Condor and Lufthansa**.
- Flight time is about **4h–4h15**.
- Condor was displaying economy fares from about **€172.99 per direction as part of a round trip** on some September dates.
- Lufthansa’s round-trip pricing was higher, around the €400s on cheaper future dates.

The Condor number is the one that makes this dangerous. Four hours and €170-ish per direction puts the Caucasus into the same mental category as “maybe I should take a long European weekend.”

## rough trip idea

### four days

**Day 1:** Cascade, city centre, dinner, wine.

**Day 2:** Republic Square, markets, museums, Kond, long café drift.

**Day 3:** Garni + Geghard day trip.

**Day 4:** one more obscene meal, Ararat view, airport.
