# Okinawa, Japan

![Kerama Islands, Okinawa](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Okinawa%20kerama%20islands.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because **Shanghai → Okinawa is about 2h40 nonstop**, and then Naha puts the Kerama Islands within day-trip range.

This is Japan behaving like a subtropical island chain: coral reefs, white beaches, bright blue water, Ryukyuan history, and a completely different atmosphere from Tokyo/Kansai Japan.

## the dangerous detail

Okinawa's official tourism guide describes the **Kerama Islands** as a cluster of inhabited and uninhabited islands with clear reef-filled water, coral, beaches, diving, snorkeling, hiking and winter humpback-whale watching — close enough to Naha for day trips.

So the route is essentially:

**Shanghai → Naha → boat → reef.**

## things I would actually want to do

- Ferry to Zamami or Tokashiki.
- Snorkel or dive in the Kerama waters.
- Spend at least one night outside central Naha.
- Eat Okinawa soba, taco rice, sea grapes, and whatever tiny izakaya looks alive.
- Learn some Ryukyuan history instead of treating Okinawa as “Japan with beaches.”
- Come in winter once just to see the whales.
