# Comox Valley, Vancouver Island

![Comox Valley from Mount Washington](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Comox%20Valley%20from%20Mt%20Washington.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because Vancouver → Comox is a cheap, short regional hop that lands between **ocean and mountains**.

Pacific Coastal's current fare pages were showing future YVR → YQQ one-way basic fares around **CAD $117** on selected dates when checked.

## what got me

The valley bundles Courtenay, Comox and Cumberland with beaches, farms, mountain biking, glacier-fed lakes, seafood and Mount Washington. Official tourism material describes the region as "alpine to ocean" geography, which is exactly the sales pitch.

In winter, Mount Washington is a real ski trip. In summer, it flips into hiking/biking while the coast stays right there.

## from Vancouver

Checked **2026-08-29**:

- Pacific Coastal sells YVR → Comox nonstop.
- Example future one-way basic fares were around **CAD $117**.
- Other carriers also serve the route; exact pricing varies wildly by date.

## what I would actually do

- Cumberland mountain biking / food.
- Mount Washington for alpine views or skiing depending on season.
- Comox waterfront and seafood.
- One long slow beach/coast afternoon.
- Use the valley as a base for north-central Vancouver Island instead of making every trip start in Victoria or Nanaimo.
