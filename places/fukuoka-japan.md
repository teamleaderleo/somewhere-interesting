# Fukuoka, Japan

![Yatai in Fukuoka](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Yatai.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because **Shanghai → Fukuoka is about 2h05 nonstop** and current schedules show roughly **three flights a day**.

That means one of Japan's best food cities is sitting there as a long-weekend option.

## the immediate hook

Fukuoka's official tourism guide leans hard into **yatai**: more than 100 evening food stalls across the city serving ramen, gyoza, yakitori, oden, tempura and other small dishes.

This is the kind of city where the itinerary can simply be:

1. walk,
2. eat,
3. keep walking,
4. discover another thing to eat.

Fukuoka is also compact enough to feel human-scale while still being a major Japanese city.

## things I would actually want to do

- Eat tonkotsu ramen at an unreasonable hour.
- Sit at several yatai instead of trying to identify the One Perfect Stall.
- Explore Hakata and Tenjin at night.
- Take an easy day trip to Dazaifu or somewhere coastal.
- Eat gomasaba, motsunabe, yakitori and whatever is on the seasonal menu.
- Use Fukuoka as the front door to Kyushu for a longer trip later.

## the Shanghai portal

Checked **2026-08-29**:

- **PVG → FUK** nonstop: about **2h05**.
- Current schedules show around **21 flights per week**.
- China Eastern and Spring Airlines currently operate nonstop service.

## rough trip

**Night 1:** arrive and immediately eat.

**Day 2:** city wander + yatai.

**Day 3:** Dazaifu / coast / more food.

**Day 4:** final bowl of ramen, airport.
