## why this is here

Because someone apparently took a Portuguese Atlantic island, made it **vertical and green as hell**, then connected it nonstop to Frankfurt in about four hours.

Madeira's appeal is immediate from the photos: mountains erupting out of the ocean, cliffs, terraced slopes, waterfalls, dense forest, villages tucked into impossible-looking terrain, and Funchal sitting between mountain and sea.

## the immediate reaction

The island's highest point, **Pico Ruivo**, reaches about **1,862 metres**. Around the island you get viewpoints over mountains, terraced fields, streams, waterfalls, jagged sea cliffs and the Atlantic.

Funchal itself is useful because this does not have to be a pure hiking expedition. Madeira's tourism board describes a city of gardens, fortresses, high viewpoints, tropical vegetation, bay views and ocean promenades.

So the itinerary can oscillate between:

> I am on top of a cloud-covered mountain.

and

> I am eating in a Portuguese city beside the Atlantic.

Excellent.

## things I would actually want to do

- Hike in the central mountains around Pico Ruivo / Pico do Arieiro.
- Walk one of the **levada** routes.
- Spend a day wandering Funchal and riding up into the hills.
- Find several viewpoints and compare how absurdly steep the island looks from each one.
- Swim in volcanic / natural pools.
- Eat espetada, seafood and too much bread.
- Leave one day open for whichever village / cliff / forest road looks most ridiculous on the map.

## the Frankfurt portal

Checked **2026-08-29**:

- Discover Airlines advertises **FRA → FNC nonstop**.
- Scheduled flight time: about **4h 15m**.
- Advertised fares on the route page started around **€286**, with sample autumn months around €302–345.

This is pricier than the Hurghada / Agadir cheap-seat chaos, but geographically it still feels offensive: four hours after Frankfurt you can be on a lush volcanic island in the Atlantic.

## rough trip idea

### five days

**Day 1:** Funchal, cable-car / viewpoint energy, dinner.

**Day 2:** big mountain day.

**Day 3:** levada walk + tiny village.

**Day 4:** coast, cliffs, pools, drive somewhere dramatic.

**Day 5:** Funchal again, gardens, food, stare at the ocean and resist opening real-estate websites.
