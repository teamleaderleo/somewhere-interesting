## why this is here

This entry began at **Gate B41 in Frankfurt Airport**.

A quiet gate suddenly filled with vacationers. The flight board said **Hurghada**. We looked it up and discovered two things at once:

1. Egypt has an entire Red Sea beach-and-diving universe that feels wildly different from the Cairo-and-pyramids picture many people carry around.
2. Frankfurt is connected enough that Germans can casually take a direct flight from FRA to that universe in about **4 hours 40 minutes**.

That was sufficient cause for a repository.

## the immediate reaction

Hurghada is what happens when Egyptian desert runs straight into startlingly blue water.

The official Egypt tourism site describes a former fishing village turned major beach destination, with resorts, coral reefs, sandy coastline, water sports, seafood, a marina, markets, and desert activities.

The nearby **Giftun Islands** are the part that really detonated the imagination: white sand, clear blue water, coral reefs, snorkeling, diving, day-use beaches, and boats running out from Hurghada.

And apparently the underwater visibility can reach **70 metres in some spots**.

This is a whole Red Sea holiday ecosystem.

## things I would actually want to do

- Take a boat to **Giftun Islands**.
- Snorkel over the coral reefs.
- Spend a day at Mahmeya / one of the island beach setups.
- Walk the marina after sunset.
- Eat seafood somewhere facing the water.
- Take a quad bike or buggy into the desert.
- Wander away from resort-land enough to see the older/local side of Hurghada.
- If the place still has its hooks in me after a few days, learn enough scuba to make the reef problem worse.

## rough trip idea

### three days

**Day 1:** arrive, beach, marina, eat something good, stare at the Red Sea.

**Day 2:** Giftun Islands boat day; snorkel until the concept of email becomes abstract.

**Day 3:** desert / old Hurghada / museum / one last swim.

### five-ish days

Add a diving course or several serious reef days and leave some time deliberately blank.
