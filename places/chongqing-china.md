# Chongqing, China

![Hongya Cave, Chongqing](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Hongya%20Cave%2020260507-1.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because **Nantong → Chongqing is about 2h35 nonstop**, with current schedules showing multiple flights per day.

This is the easiest possible answer to “I want a city that feels completely unlike Jiangsu.”

## the city

Chongqing is rivers, hills, bridges, towers, hotpot steam and buildings stacked on top of other buildings until your sense of street level stops being useful.

Hongya Cave is the photogenic obvious bit, but the larger appeal is the vertical city itself: metro lines through mountains and buildings, staircases that are functionally streets, the Yangtze and Jialing meeting below you, and a nightscape that looks like somebody gave urbanism a gaming PC.

## things I would actually want to do

- Eat Chongqing hotpot until the table becomes a weather system.
- Wander Hongya Cave and the riverside after dark.
- Ride the metro specifically for strange elevation changes.
- Take the Yangtze River cableway.
- Get lost between upper and lower street levels.
- Spend a morning somewhere quieter so the entire trip isn't neon stimulation.
- Add Dazu Rock Carvings on a longer visit.
