## why this is here

Because Almaty looks like a proper city that accidentally spawned at the foot of the **Tian Shan**.

This is the part that breaks my brain: the local tourism site literally pitches it as **a city with the mountains about 30 minutes away**. You can have cafés, nightlife and city streets, then be on a cable car climbing into alpine terrain before the day has had time to become boring.

## the thing that got me

The mountain access is outrageous.

**Medeu** sits high above the city, and from there a cable car climbs to **Shymbulak**, whose resort levels run roughly 2,260–3,200 metres above sea level. In winter: skiing. In summer: trails, mountain terraces, panoramas and the recurring question of why your ordinary city has a 3,000-metre playground attached to it.

Then there is Big Almaty Lake, canyon trips, road trips, hiking, paragliding and the wider Zailiysky Alatau range.

Almaty also has the human side we care about: cafés, bars, art spaces, Soviet-era city layers, leafy streets and Central Asian food waiting underneath the mountain drama.

## things I would actually want to do

- Spend a day wandering Almaty with no mountain agenda at all.
- Take the cable car from Medeu toward Shymbulak.
- Hike somewhere high enough that the city feels theoretical.
- Eat aggressively: beshbarmak, manty, lagman, samsa, whatever crosses the table.
- Do a canyon day trip.
- Sit in a café after the mountains and experience the strange luxury of having both in the same day.

## the Frankfurt portal

Checked **2026-08-29**:

- FRA → ALA has nonstop service from **Lufthansa and Air Astana**.
- Typical nonstop time is roughly **7h05–7h25** depending on flight.
- Air Astana was showing September round-trip economy fares from about **€694** on cheaper dates.
- Lufthansa also sells the nonstop route, with similar future pricing in the high hundreds on many dates.

So it costs more than our beach portals, but Frankfurt can still put you **directly into Central Asia with the Tian Shan looming over the city**.

## rough trip idea

### six days

**Day 1:** Almaty city, food, parks, cafés.

**Day 2:** Medeu + Shymbulak.

**Day 3:** mountain hike.

**Day 4:** canyon / road-trip day.

**Day 5:** city again — museums, markets, bars, absolutely excessive dinner.

**Day 6:** one final mountain look before airport.
