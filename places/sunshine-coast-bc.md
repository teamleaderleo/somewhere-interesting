# Sunshine Coast, British Columbia

![Sechelt area](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Sechelt%20area.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

The Sunshine Coast is attached to mainland BC and still behaves like an island: the official tourism board describes a **180 km coast accessible only by ferry, boat or plane**.

That gives it a very satisfying escape mechanic from Vancouver.

## the Vancouver trick

Harbour Air's 2026 network includes **YVR South → Sechelt**. So the family option is either:

- ferry/road and let the trip unfold slowly, or
- small plane and appear on the coast absurdly quickly.

## what got me

Gibsons, Roberts Creek, Sechelt, Skookumchuck Narrows, Desolation Sound, mountain-bike parks, long trails, inlets and artist communities. It is close to Vancouver without feeling like suburban Vancouver escaped north.

## what I would actually do

- Seaplane in, ferry back — maximum transportation variety.
- Gibsons + Roberts Creek + Sechelt for a short trip.
- Go farther north for Skookumchuck Narrows / Powell River / Desolation Sound when there is more time.
- Eat seafood and sit somewhere facing water for longer than is efficient.
