# Xi'an, Shaanxi

![Xi'an city wall](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/City%20wall%20of%20Xi%27an.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because Nantong has current nonstop service to Xi'an in about **2h20**.

That means one ordinary Jiangsu airport hop gets you to a former imperial capital, the eastern end of the ancient Silk Road, the Terracotta Warriors, city walls, Tang-era sites and an entirely different food universe.

## what got me

Xi'an served as the capital of multiple Chinese dynasties and was known historically as Chang'an. The city government highlights the Terracotta Warriors, Xi'an City Wall, Giant Wild Goose Pagoda and Tang cultural areas as major draws.

And the city wall is still a giant physical object you can walk or bike around rather than a decorative fragment.

## what I would actually do

- Terracotta Warriors with enough time to actually look rather than photograph-and-run.
- Walk/bike the city wall near sunset.
- Eat roujiamo, liangpi and paomo.
- Giant Wild Goose Pagoda + Tang-night-city spectacle.
- Give the city at least three days.
