# Kelowna + the Okanagan, British Columbia

![Okanagan Lake, Kelowna](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Okanagan%20Lake%2C%20Kelowna%2C%20BC%2C%20Canada.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because Vancouver → Kelowna can be **cheap enough to feel like transit**, while the destination flips you into lakes, vineyards, orchards and dry interior hills.

Air Canada's current fare calendar was showing some future YVR → YLW one-way economy fares around **CAD $62–73** on selected dates when checked.

## what got me

Tourism Kelowna describes a city wrapped around Okanagan Lake, vineyards, orchards and a semi-arid valley climate. It is also a UNESCO City of Gastronomy.

This is probably the easiest family trip on the Vancouver list where everyone can choose a different vacation: wine, beaches, food, hiking, cycling, lake time.

## from Vancouver

Checked **2026-08-29**:

- YVR → YLW has very frequent nonstop service.
- Air Canada was advertising selected future one-way fares from roughly **CAD $62**.
- Flight time is about an hour.

## what I would actually do

- Get a car.
- Stay near the lake but spend real time outside central Kelowna.
- Winery / orchard day.
- Beach / swim day in summer.
- Drive farther down the valley if time allows rather than pretending Kelowna is the whole Okanagan.
