# Huangshan, Anhui

![Huangshan](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Huangshan%2C%20China%20%28YELLOW%20MOUNTAIN-LANDSCAPE%29%20V%20%281051997868%29.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because Shanghai can put you in **the canonical Chinese mountain-painting landscape in about three hours by train**.

UNESCO describes Mount Huangshan as granite peaks and ancient pines emerging from a sea of clouds, a landscape that helped feed centuries of Chinese shanshui painting and poetry.

## what got me

This is one of those places where the clichés are clichés because the actual place is absurd: vertical granite, twisted pines, cloud inversions, stone stairways, hot springs and viewpoints that look painted even when photographed badly.

## what I would actually do

- Take an early train from Shanghai.
- Sleep on or near the mountain instead of speed-running it.
- Catch sunrise and, with luck, a sea of clouds.
- Spend a second day walking rather than stacking cable cars.
- Pair it with an old Anhui village if time allows.
