# Jeju, South Korea

![Hallasan on Jeju](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Jeju%20Hallasan.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because **Shanghai → Jeju is about 1 hour 35 minutes nonstop**.

That is an absurd amount of island for a flight shorter than many people's commute-plus-airport nonsense.

Jeju is volcanic South Korea: Hallasan in the middle, lava tubes under the ground, black rock coast, beaches, tangerines, seafood, wind, and villages scattered around the island.

## the big thing

**Hallasan** rises to 1,950 m and is the highest mountain in South Korea. Jeju's official tourism site highlights Hallasan together with the Geomunoreum lava-tube system and Seongsan Ilchulbong as the island's UNESCO volcanic-landscape core.

This is the kind of trip where you can hike a volcano one day and eat seafood beside the ocean the next.

## things I would actually want to do

- Hike Hallasan or at least one of its shorter trails.
- Catch sunrise at Seongsan Ilchulbong.
- Explore the lava-tube landscape.
- Drive the coast with no heroic itinerary.
- Eat black pork, abalone, seafood stew, and too many tangerine things.
- Find a quiet beach or little village away from the main tourist crush.

## the Shanghai portal

Checked **2026-08-29**:

- **PVG → CJU** nonstop: about **1h35**.
- Multiple airlines currently operate the route, including China Eastern, Spring, Juneyao, Jin Air and Eastar Jet.

This is a textbook **three-night family sidequest**.

## rough trip

**Day 1:** arrive, coast, food, sleep.

**Day 2:** Hallasan / serious nature day.

**Day 3:** Seongsan + east coast + seafood.

**Day 4:** lazy morning and fly home before the trip acquires administrative weight.
