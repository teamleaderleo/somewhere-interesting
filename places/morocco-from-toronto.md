# Morocco from Toronto

![Jemaa el-Fna, Marrakech](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Maroc%20Marrakech%20Jemaa-el-Fna%20Luc%20Viatour.JPG?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because Toronto Pearson → Casablanca is currently **7h25 nonstop**, three times a week on Royal Air Maroc.

Seven-ish hours buys a new continent, Arabic and Amazigh culture, Atlantic cities, medinas, riads, mountains, desert-country possibilities and a food universe that can carry an entire trip by itself.

## the wait-what moment

Checked **2026-09-04**:

- YYZ → CMN nonstop: about **7h25**.
- Current schedule: **3 flights/week**.
- Royal Air Maroc is the nonstop operator.

Casablanca is useful as the front door even when it is only the first stop. The obvious branches are **Rabat / Fez**, **Marrakech / Atlas Mountains**, **Essaouira**, or a longer route toward southeastern desert landscapes.

## things worth doing

- Spend enough time in Casablanca to see the Hassan II Mosque and the city's Art Deco / Atlantic layers.
- Take the train toward Rabat or Fez if history and old-city wandering is the priority.
- Use Marrakech for riads, souks, food and an Atlas extension.
- Add Essaouira if the trip needs sea air and a slower rhythm.
- Let Moroccan food be itinerary content: tagine, couscous, bread, grilled meats, pastries, mint tea, markets.

## rough trip idea

**5 nights:** Casablanca + Marrakech, with one focused day trip.

**8–10 nights:** Casablanca/Rabat → Fez → Marrakech, or Marrakech + Atlas + Essaouira.
