# Whitehorse, Yukon

![Northern lights in Whitehorse](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Northern%20Lights%20in%20Whitehorse.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because Vancouver has **multiple direct flights to Whitehorse in a day**.

The Canadian North sounds huge and abstract until YVR's departure board casually says WHITEHORSE several times between breakfast and dinner.

## the place

Travel Yukon calls Whitehorse the **Wilderness City**: the Yukon River runs through town, hundreds of kilometres of trail spread around it, floatplanes leave from Schwatka Lake, and hot springs / wildlife / mountains sit within easy driving range.

And the aurora season starts around **late August** and runs into spring.

So Vancouver can go from coastal city to northern-lights base in roughly a couple of hours.

## things I would actually want to do

- See the aurora from outside city light.
- Hike Miles Canyon beside that ridiculous turquoise water.
- Soak at Eclipse Nordic Hot Springs.
- Paddle a stretch of the Yukon River.
- Drive toward Carcross / Emerald Lake.
- Spend a day learning more about Yukon First Nations history and contemporary life.
- Come back in a different season because summer and winter are basically different planets.

## the Vancouver portal

Checked **2026-08-29**:

- YVR's current departure board shows **multiple direct Air North and Air Canada flights to Whitehorse**.
- Typical nonstop flying time is around **2h20-ish**.

This is one of those routes that changes the mental map of Canada.

## rough trip

**Three nights:** enough for a first aurora-oriented sidequest.

**Five days:** Whitehorse + trails + hot springs + Carcross / Southern Lakes.

**A week+:** start using Whitehorse as the door to Kluane and a proper Yukon road trip.
