# Guilin + Yangshuo, Guangxi

![Li River near Yangshuo](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Li%20River%2C%20Yangshuo%2C%20China.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because Nantong now has a route to Guilin in roughly **2h30**.

And Guilin/Yangshuo is one of those landscapes that looks like somebody turned the saturation slider on Chinese ink painting: karst towers, rivers, bamboo, bikes, caves and villages.

## from Nantong

Checked **2026-08-29**:

- NTG → KWL nonstop is listed at about **2h30**.
- Current route data shows a small number of weekly nonstop flights, so this one needs date-checking more than Chengdu or Xi'an.

## what I would actually do

- Land in Guilin and keep moving toward Yangshuo instead of spending the whole trip in the city.
- Li River / Yulong River day.
- Bike or scooter through the karst countryside.
- Stay somewhere with mountains outside the window.
- Give it enough time that mist/rain becomes atmosphere rather than a ruined plan.
