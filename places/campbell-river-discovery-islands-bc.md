# Campbell River + Discovery Islands, BC

![Campbell River Harbour](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Campbell%20River%20Harbour%2C%20Vancouver%20Island%2C%20British%20Columbia%2C%20Canada.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because **Vancouver → Campbell River is about 46 minutes nonstop** from YVR South, and Campbell River sits directly beside Discovery Passage with the Discovery Islands spreading out beyond it.

The drive/ferry version feels like a Vancouver Island trip. The little-plane version feels like somebody installed a shortcut.

## the wait-what moment

Checked **2026-09-04**:

- Pacific Coastal advertises **year-round double-daily nonstop service** between Vancouver YVR South and Campbell River.
- Current schedule data puts the nonstop flight at about **46 minutes**.
- Current advance basic fares on selected dates were showing from roughly **CAD $129 one way** on Pacific Coastal's own fare page.

Then the destination immediately turns marine: Discovery Passage, Quadra Island, the wider Discovery Islands, whales, dolphins, sea lions, eagles, bears and fishing-country history.

This is another case where the regional airline changes the entire category of the trip.

## what it seems like

Campbell River is a working coastal city with mountains across the water and boats constantly suggesting somewhere else to go.

The interesting part radiates outward: **Quadra Island, Discovery Islands, wildlife trips, Strathcona Provincial Park, salmon rivers, channels and inlets**.

Destination Campbell River's own tourism material leans heavily into wildlife viewing — whales, dolphins, sea lions, bears, elk and eagles — because the city works as a marine-access hub as much as a destination by itself.

## things worth doing

- Get on the water: whale / wildlife tour, preferably with a strong naturalist component.
- Ferry to **Quadra Island** and let one trip become two places.
- Eat seafood and watch the working harbour do its thing.
- Look into Indigenous cultural experiences and learn the **Liǧʷiłdax̌ʷ** context of the region rather than treating the islands as empty wilderness.
- If the trip grows, add **Strathcona Provincial Park** or continue farther into the Discovery Islands.
- Go in salmon season if the timing, regulations and local operator advice line up with what we want to see.

## from Vancouver

This may be the cleanest tiny-plane argument after Tofino.

**YVR South → 46 minutes → Campbell River.**

For a 2–3 night trip, that saves enough time and friction to change whether the trip happens at all.

## rough trip idea

**2 nights:**

1. Fly YVR → YBL; harbour / seafood / easy waterfront evening.
2. Full wildlife or Discovery Islands boat day.
3. Quadra Island morning or short coastal outing; fly home.

**4 nights:** add Strathcona / deeper island time and make Campbell River the hinge between mountains and sea.
