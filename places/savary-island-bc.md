# Savary Island, BC

## why this is here

Because somebody says “tiny BC island” and then the photos show **long pale-sand beaches and clear green water** that look wildly out of character for the latitude.

Savary is a particularly good repo destination because getting there is just inconvenient enough to feel like an adventure, while the shortcut through Powell River keeps the trip sane.

## the wait-what moment

The island has **no bridge and no car ferry**. Most visitors reach it by water taxi from Lund.

Checked **2026-09-04**:

- Vancouver → Powell River can be flown nonstop in about **36 minutes**.
- Powell River → Lund is roughly another half-hour by road.
- Lund → Savary is about a **10-minute water-taxi crossing**.
- Lund Water Taxi's 2026 scheduled fare is listed at **CAD $16 one way per adult**.

So the ridiculous sequence is:

**Vancouver airport → tiny plane → end of Highway 101 → ten-minute boat → sandy island.**

## what it seems like

Summer-cottage BC with the visual dial turned strangely tropical.

Savary is narrow, sandy and low-lying, with dirt roads, forest, dunes and beaches wrapping much of the island. There is no need to manufacture an itinerary. The point is to bike or walk, swim, read, eat, wander down roads until they hit water, and stay for sunset.

The limited transport is part of the charm. Pack with some intention and then quit moving so much.

## things worth doing

- Pick a beach and spend a deeply unproductive afternoon there.
- Bike the island's dirt roads.
- Walk long stretches of shore at a tide that makes the beach generous.
- Bring food / supplies with island logistics in mind.
- Stay overnight; the island makes much more sense after the day-trippers leave.
- Pair it with [Powell River / qathet](powell-river-qathet-bc.md) rather than treating the transfer as dead time.

## getting there

The cleanest family-orbit version is:

1. Fly YVR → Powell River.
2. Taxi / rental / ride north to Lund.
3. Water taxi to Savary.
4. Use an island land taxi if the accommodation is far from the wharf.

The ferry-road version from Vancouver is also possible and considerably more elaborate.

Exact water-taxi schedules and baggage rules change, so this one deserves a fresh logistics check every time.

## rough trip idea

**3 nights:** one night Powell River/Lund + two nights Savary.

**4–5 nights:** Savary gets enough time to become actual island life instead of a photography mission.
