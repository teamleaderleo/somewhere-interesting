# Xiamen, Fujian, China

![Gulangyu](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Gulangyu.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because **Nantong → Xiamen is about 1h50 nonstop**.

That is barely enough time to get properly bored on a plane, and then you are in subtropical Fujian beside the sea.

## the hook: Gulangyu

UNESCO describes **Kulangsu / Gulangyu** as a tiny island facing Xiamen whose history as an international settlement produced a genuinely strange architectural mixture: Southern Fujian styles, Western classical revival, veranda colonial buildings, and an “Amoy Deco” hybrid.

The island contains **hundreds of historic buildings**, gardens and a dense pedestrian street network.

So the pitch is less “generic coastal city” and more:

**warm seafront city + little historical island + Fujian food + absurdly short flight from Nantong.**

## things I would actually want to do

- Ferry to Gulangyu and wander without trying to optimize it.
- Stay through early morning or evening when day-trip density drops.
- Eat seafood and Fujian dishes.
- Walk Xiamen's waterfront and older neighbourhoods.
- Use Xiamen as a base for a future Fujian / tulou trip.
