# Iceland from Toronto

![Gullfoss, Iceland](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Gullfoss%20rainbow.JPG?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because Toronto → Keflavík is currently about **5h35 nonstop**, with roughly **17 flights/week** in the September schedule.

That is barely enough time to settle into a long-haul mindset before the plane lands among lava fields.

## the wait-what moment

Checked **2026-09-04**:

- YYZ → KEF nonstop: about **5h35**.
- Current market: Air Canada + Icelandair.
- Current schedule: roughly **17 flights/week**.

From Reykjavík/Keflavík, the famous easy first loop already contains **Þingvellir, Geysir and Gullfoss**. South Iceland then adds glaciers, black-sand coast and waterfalls, while a longer trip can push toward Snæfellsnes or farther around the Ring Road.

## things worth doing

- Spend at least one day in Reykjavík rather than sprinting straight into a rental car.
- Do the Golden Circle once; popularity does not make Gullfoss less enormous.
- Go along the South Coast for waterfalls, black beach and glacier country.
- Use geothermal pools as ordinary daily life, not merely one expensive famous lagoon.
- Visit outside peak summer if the dream involves aurora, snow or fewer people.

## rough trip idea

**4 nights:** Reykjavík + Golden Circle + South Coast.

**7–10 nights:** road trip far enough that Reykjavík stops being the centre of gravity.
