# Mexico City

![Palacio de Bellas Artes, Mexico City](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Bellas%20Artes%2001.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because Toronto → Mexico City is currently one of the easiest giant changes of scene on the board: **about 25 nonstops a week**, with the quickest flight around **4h40**.

Then you land more than 2,000 metres above sea level in a megacity built over the world of Tenochtitlan, with neighbourhood food, museums, murals, markets and volcano country beyond the urban basin.

## the wait-what moment

Checked **2026-09-04**:

- YYZ → MEX currently runs about **25 times/week** nonstop.
- Aeromexico, Air Canada and Flair appear in the current nonstop market.
- Fastest scheduled flight is about **4h40**.

The archaeological zone of **Teotihuacan** sits outside the city and preserves the monumental core of an ancient metropolis that once held somewhere between 100,000 and 200,000 people.

So a single long weekend can contain contemporary Mexico City, Mexica history and a much older city of enormous pyramids.

## things worth doing

- Walk the Centro Histórico and start around the Zócalo.
- Spend serious museum time: Anthropology is the obvious heavyweight.
- Eat across neighbourhoods instead of making restaurants separate from sightseeing.
- See murals and public art.
- Give Teotihuacan half a day or more.
- Leave enough time for one neighbourhood day with almost no agenda.

## rough trip idea

**4 nights:** historic centre, Chapultepec/museums, food/neighbourhood day, Teotihuacan.

**6–7 nights:** add Xochimilco, more museums, markets and a slower neighbourhood rhythm.
