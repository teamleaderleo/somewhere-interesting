# Hangzhou, Zhejiang

![West Lake panorama](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/West%20Lake%20IMG%208755%20hangzhou%20panorama.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

Hangzhou is the first reminder that **Shanghai's rail radius is ridiculous**.

Current 2026 timetables show Shanghai ↔ Hangzhou high-speed trains taking as little as roughly **32 minutes**, with hundreds of departures on busy days. This is barely a trip in transportation terms, yet West Lake immediately changes the atmosphere.

## what got me

West Lake is a UNESCO-listed cultural landscape wrapped into a modern city: causeways, pagodas, gardens, boats, tea country and wooded hills sitting beside a major urban centre.

Hangzhou's own tourism material calls out the Su Causeway, Leifeng Pagoda, Three Pools Mirroring the Moon and the lake's walking/biking network.

## what I would actually do

- Walk or bike a long arc around West Lake instead of treating it like a photo stop.
- Take a boat at dusk.
- Go uphill toward tea country / Longjing.
- Eat Hangzhou food somewhere that feels older than the giant malls.
- Stay one night so the lake gets an evening and early-morning version.

## from Shanghai

Checked **2026-08-29**:

- High-speed rail: roughly **32m–1h-ish** on the quickest services, depending on station/train.
- Service is extremely frequent.
- This is an easy day trip, but an overnight makes more sense if the point is to slow down.
