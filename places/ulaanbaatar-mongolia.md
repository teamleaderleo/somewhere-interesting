# Ulaanbaatar + Terelj, Mongolia

![Ulaanbaatar skyline](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Ulaanbaatar%20skyline.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because Shanghai now has a direct low-cost flight to Ulaanbaatar, and four-ish hours after leaving Pudong you can be in Mongolia.

That is a pleasingly aggressive change of scenery.

## the wait-what moment

Checked **2026-09-04**:

- Spring Airlines launched **Shanghai Pudong → Ulaanbaatar nonstop** on 2026-03-03.
- The launch schedule was Tuesday / Friday; the airline's current timetable shows additional seasonal operating days in parts of summer 2026.
- Flight time is about **4 hours**.

The better part is what sits right outside the capital: **Gorkhi-Terelj National Park is about 60 km east of Ulaanbaatar**, roughly an hour by road according to Ulaanbaatar's official tourism material.

So the route can be used as city + immediate countryside rather than “fly to Mongolia and then plan an expedition.”

## what it seems like

Ulaanbaatar gives the trip its urban / historical beginning: monasteries, museums, Soviet-era planning, cafés and the energy of a capital that contains a huge share of the country's population.

Then the road opens into granite hills, grass valleys, horses and ger camps.

The appeal is the transition itself: **Shanghai megacity → Ulaanbaatar → sleep in a ger** inside a long weekend / short break time budget.

## things worth doing

- Spend a proper day in Ulaanbaatar rather than treating it only as an airport.
- Visit **Gandan Monastery** and one or two of the stronger national-history museums.
- Drive out to **Gorkhi-Terelj National Park**.
- Stay overnight in a **ger camp**.
- Hike around the granite formations / valleys and visit Aryabal meditation temple if it fits the route.
- Horse riding with a reputable local operator.
- Add Hustai or a farther steppe loop when the trip grows past four days.

## rough trip idea

**4 nights:**

1. PVG → UBN; Ulaanbaatar evening.
2. Ulaanbaatar city day.
3. Drive to Terelj; hike / ride / ger overnight.
4. Slow Terelj morning, return to UB.
5. Fly home.

That is already enough to make the trip feel enormous compared with the actual flight time.
