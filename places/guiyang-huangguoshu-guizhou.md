# Guiyang + Huangguoshu, Guizhou

![Huangguoshu Waterfall](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Huangguoshu%20dapubu.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because Nantong → Guiyang is currently about **2h55 nonstop**, with multiple airlines on the route, and the obvious extension is one of China's great waterfall-and-karst landscapes.

This is another NTG trick where the destination feels dramatically farther away than the clock says.

## the wait-what moment

Checked **2026-09-04**:

- NTG → KWE is currently nonstop.
- Current schedule data shows **four airlines** serving the route, with multiple daily departures across the active schedule.
- Published flight time is about **2h55**.

Then there is **Huangguoshu** near Anshun: a huge karst waterfall system with caves, gorges, clustered falls and Buyi villages. UNESCO's tentative-list material describes the main Huangguoshu fall as about **77.8 m high and 101 m wide**, within a much larger stepped waterfall landscape.

So the proposition is pleasantly simple:

**Nantong → three-hour flight → Guizhou food / karst / giant waterfall.**

## what it seems like

Guiyang is the soft landing: highland city, Guizhou food, markets, parks, spicy-sour flavours, and a base for a province where the landscape gets dramatic quickly.

Huangguoshu is the visual payoff. The main waterfall gets the famous photos, but the wider scenic area is a karst system of waterfalls, caves, rivers and villages rather than a single photo stop.

This feels strongest as a 3–5 day trip where Guiyang supplies the food/city layer and Anshun/Huangguoshu supplies the giant-water layer.

## things worth doing

- Eat through Guiyang before disappearing into scenic areas: sour soup, rice noodles, grilled / street-food territory, whatever local relatives or taxi drivers insist on.
- Give **Huangguoshu** a full day rather than treating the main cascade as a quick stop.
- Walk the **Water-Curtain Cave** route behind the waterfall if access conditions cooperate.
- Explore another part of the waterfall cluster / karst landscape instead of spending the whole trip at one viewpoint.
- Look into a **Buyi village** or another cultural stop with enough time to treat it as a living place.
- If the trip grows, use Guiyang as a launchpad for a much deeper Guizhou rabbit hole.

## from Nantong

The route is strong enough to graduate from the research queue.

The broader lesson is that NTG does not only provide obvious big-city hops. It can also place us directly into the Guizhou highlands in the same time budget as many ordinary domestic weekends.

## rough trip idea

**3 nights:**

1. NTG → KWE; Guiyang food evening.
2. Guiyang / local food / city day, or head toward Anshun.
3. Full Huangguoshu day.
4. Slow morning and fly home.

**5 nights:** add another Guizhou landscape / village stop and let the trip become a proper provincial loop.
