# Powell River / qathet, BC

## why this is here

Because Vancouver → Powell River is currently about **36 minutes nonstop**.

The ground version involves the Sunshine Coast ferry chain and enough logistics to make the place feel remote. The little-plane version is shorter than many commutes and drops you onto the northern Sunshine Coast beside lakes, forest, coastline and the gateway to Lund.

## the wait-what moment

Checked **2026-09-04**:

- YVR → YPW nonstop: about **36 minutes**.
- Current schedule: roughly **29 flights/week**.
- Pacific Coastal is the nonstop operator.
- Selected future basic fares were showing around **CAD $107 one way** when checked.

Powell River then opens several different trips at once: the **Powell Forest Canoe Route**, sections of the Sunshine Coast Trail, diving, lakes, old townsite history, Lund at the end of Highway 101, and onward water access toward Savary Island and Desolation Sound.

## what it seems like

A place where the transport hack is half the revelation.

From Vancouver, qathet can look like a long ferry-and-road undertaking. Flying turns it into a casual coastal base with enough surrounding wilderness to fill a week.

This is also a good trip for leaving blank space: lake day, trail day, seafood, drive to Lund, stare at the water, decide to get on another boat.

## things worth doing

- Walk part of the **Sunshine Coast Trail** instead of feeling obliged to tackle all of it.
- Paddle a piece of the **Powell Forest Canoe Route** if the logistics fit.
- Explore the historic Townsite and waterfront.
- Drive north to **Lund**, literally the end of Highway 101.
- Use Lund as the jumping-off point for [Savary Island](savary-island-bc.md).
- Look at Desolation Sound boat / kayak options if the trip grows.

## from Vancouver

This is peak homebody economics.

**YVR South → 36 minutes → northern Sunshine Coast.**

The flight solves so much friction that a place which feels like a road-trip project becomes viable for two or three nights.

## rough trip idea

**2 nights:** fly up, coastal / Townsite afternoon, full trail/lake day, Lund morning, fly home.

**4 nights:** add Savary or a proper paddling day and let the trip spread out.
