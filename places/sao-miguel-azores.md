# São Miguel, Azores

![Sete Cidades, São Miguel](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Sete%20Cidades%20in%20Sao%20Miguel%2C%20Azores.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because Toronto Pearson → Ponta Delgada is currently about **5h45 nonstop**, and the island at the other end contains crater lakes, steaming volcanic valleys, black basalt coast, hydrangeas and whale-country Atlantic.

That is an extremely high scenery return for one overnight flight.

## the wait-what moment

Checked **2026-09-04**:

- YYZ → PDL currently has about **9 nonstop flights/week**.
- Air Canada, Azores Airlines and WestJet all appear in the current nonstop market.
- Fastest scheduled flight is about **5h45**.

São Miguel is the largest island in the Azores. The island's easy visual sales pitch is **Sete Cidades**, where twin lakes sit inside a giant volcanic caldera. Then **Furnas** adds boiling springs, fumaroles, geothermal bathing and food cooked using volcanic heat.

## things worth doing

- Drive the Sete Cidades rim and stop repeatedly because the road keeps producing ridiculous views.
- Spend a day around Furnas: fumaroles, lake, hot pools and cozido.
- Follow the north/east coast for cliffs, small towns and waterfalls.
- Go whale watching if season and sea conditions line up.
- Eat Azorean beef, fish, limpets and pineapple while pretending this is a normal five-hour flight from Toronto.

## rough trip idea

**4 nights:** Ponta Delgada → Sete Cidades → Furnas → east/north coast → home.

**6–7 nights:** slow the driving down, add hikes and hot springs, and let the island stop feeling like a checklist.
