# Putuoshan, Zhejiang

![Puji Temple, Mount Putuo](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Puji%20Temple%20in%20Mount%20Putuo.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because one of China's four sacred Buddhist mountains is actually an **island off the Zhejiang coast**, close enough to Shanghai to be a family-weekend proposition.

## what got me

Mount Putuo is associated with Guanyin. The island mixes temples, incense, wooded paths, cliffs and sea. The important part is the combination: pilgrimage mountain energy, except the mountain is surrounded by water.

## getting there from Shanghai

There is no railway onto the island itself.

A common route is:

**Shanghai → Ningbo by high-speed rail → bus/car to Zhujiajian Wharf → short ferry to Putuoshan.**

Current transport guides also describe flying into Zhoushan Putuoshan Airport, then transferring to the wharf and ferry.

So this has slightly more friction than Hangzhou, but in a good way: the ferry creates an actual threshold between ordinary life and the island.

## what I would actually do

- Arrive early enough to walk rather than shuttle between every temple.
- Puji Temple.
- Fayu Temple.
- Coastal paths and viewpoints.
- Stay overnight after day-trippers thin out.
- Wake up early for the island-before-the-crowd version.
