# Tofino, British Columbia

![Cox Bay, Tofino](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Tofino%20CoxBay.JPG?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because Vancouver has direct flights to Tofino, and in summer 2026 some schedules are **daily or better**.

That turns one of BC's iconic long-drive/ferry destinations into a tiny-plane sidequest.

## the place

Tofino sits on Vancouver Island's wild west coast among beaches, rainforest and islands.

Tourism Tofino calls it Canada's surfing capital, with more than **35 km of beaches** and year-round surf. The water stays cold — wetsuit territory — while the surrounding rainforest, storms, seafood and coastline give the whole place a dramatic Pacific edge.

And then there is **Hot Springs Cove**: boat or small plane through islands, a rainforest boardwalk, then naturally heated mineral pools beside the ocean.

## things I would actually want to do

- Surf badly and joyfully at Cox Bay.
- Walk Long Beach in miserable dramatic rain and call it excellent.
- Eat seafood somewhere warm afterward.
- Do Hot Springs Cove.
- Whale / wildlife watch if the season lines up.
- Spend a day with almost no itinerary besides beach + rainforest + coffee.

## the Vancouver portal

Checked **2026-08-29**:

- YVR has direct service to **Tofino Harbour (YTP)** and **Tofino/Long Beach (YAZ)**.
- Current summer schedules show daily Harbour Air service on some dates and frequent Pacific Coastal flights to YAZ.
- Flying time is roughly **45 minutes to 1h10**, depending on airport/service.

This is almost offensively easy compared with driving.
