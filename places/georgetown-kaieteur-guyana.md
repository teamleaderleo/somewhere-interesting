# Georgetown + Kaieteur Falls, Guyana

![Kaieteur Falls](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Kaieteur%20Falls%20Aerial%2001.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because Toronto → Georgetown currently has **nonstop service**, and from Georgetown a little plane can carry you over an hour of continuous rainforest to a 226-metre single-drop waterfall.

This is exactly the kind of destination name that can appear in a flight search and quietly lead to something outrageous.

## the wait-what moment

Checked **2026-09-04**:

- YYZ → GEO currently runs about **5 times/week** nonstop.
- Current nonstop operators include Caribbean Airlines, with Air Transat service appearing from October in the current schedule.
- Fastest direct flight is about **6h15**.

Then **Kaieteur**: Guyana's official tourism site describes a 226 m / 741 ft unbroken drop deep in rainforest. The classic visit is a roughly **one-hour light-aircraft flight from Georgetown**, landing on the plateau a short walk from the falls.

## things worth doing

- Give Georgetown a real day: markets, food, wooden architecture and the country's cultural mix.
- Fly to Kaieteur and enjoy the fact that the approach itself is part of the spectacle.
- Look for the tiny golden rocket frog in giant bromeliads near the falls with a guide.
- Keep a second wilderness experience in mind if the trip grows: river, rainforest lodge or Rupununi.
- Eat Guyanese food aggressively; the country is a collision of Indigenous, African, Indian, Chinese, Portuguese and Caribbean influences.

## rough trip idea

**4 nights:** Georgetown day, Kaieteur day, another nature/food day, buffer.

**7+ nights:** add a rainforest lodge or Rupununi extension and turn the trip into something much bigger.
