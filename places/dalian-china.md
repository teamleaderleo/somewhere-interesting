# Dalian, Liaoning

![Xinghai Square, Dalian](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Dalian%20Xinghai%20Square.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because Nantong → Dalian is currently about **1h40 nonstop**.

That is a very short hop to a northern coastal city with beaches, cliffs, seafood, broad waterfront spaces and a different climate/city texture from Jiangsu.

## from Nantong

Checked **2026-08-29**:

- NTG → DLC nonstop: about **1h40**.
- Current route data shows roughly **24 flights/week** in the active season.

## what I would actually do

- Walk the Xinghai Bay waterfront.
- Eat an unreasonable quantity of seafood.
- Follow the coast rather than treating this as a downtown-only city break.
- Use it as a summer heat-escape option from the lower Yangtze region.
