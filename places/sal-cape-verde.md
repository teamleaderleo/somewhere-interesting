## why this is here

Because **Cape Verde is an island country sitting out in the Atlantic off West Africa**, and Frankfurt Airport will apparently just put you on a plane to it.

Sal is the dry, beach-heavy version of the archipelago: pale sand, turquoise water, trade winds, salt flats, low desert-like terrain and the resort town of Santa Maria.

The visual contradiction is excellent: **arid African island + absurd blue Atlantic**.

## the immediate reaction

Cape Verde's official tourism site calls Sal the country's most sought-after island and describes the **Bay of Santa Maria** as roughly **4 km of white sand and turquoise water**.

The same tourism authority points to the islands' warm, dry climate and trade winds as ideal for water sports: surfing, windsurfing, kitesurfing, diving, fishing and swimming.

So Sal's pitch is beautifully uncomplicated:

> come here, get windblown, get wet, stare across the Atlantic.

It also feels geographically weird in exactly the way this repo likes. You're west of continental Africa, on a small Portuguese-speaking island country, with ocean in every direction and a landscape that can look almost lunar a few kilometres away from the beach.

## things I would actually want to do

- Spend a whole day on Santa Maria beach with no respectable objective.
- Try kitesurfing or windsurfing.
- Dive / snorkel if the conditions are good.
- Go inland to the salt flats and the drier, stranger side of the island.
- Walk Santa Maria in the evening.
- Eat Cape Verdean food and investigate cachupa aggressively.
- Look at a map from the island and appreciate how much Atlantic surrounds you.

## the Frankfurt portal

Checked **2026-08-29**:

- Condor sells **FRA → SID (Sal)** itineraries.
- The route page was showing upcoming economy fares around **€344.99** for an October example.
- Condor notes that displayed fares may be calculated one-way as part of a round trip and can change.

So this is a bigger commitment than Hurghada or Agadir, but also a bigger geographical jump: Germany → a small Atlantic island nation off West Africa.

## rough trip idea

### five days

**Day 1:** Santa Maria, beach, walk around town, eat.

**Day 2:** water-sport experiment.

**Day 3:** salt flats / inland Sal / island loop.

**Day 4:** diving, snorkeling or boat day.

**Day 5:** achieve nothing whatsoever on the beach and call it cultural immersion.
