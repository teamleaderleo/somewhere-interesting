# Chengdu, Sichuan

![Panda in Chengdu](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Panda%20Chengdu.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because Nantong currently has **nonstop Chengdu service in about 2h55**, with dozens of flights per week.

And Chengdu is a dangerously complete family-trip city: pandas, Sichuan food, tea houses, parks, old streets, nightlife, and easy add-ons toward Dujiangyan / Qingchengshan.

## what got me

The Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding is a major conservation, research and visitor site devoted to giant pandas and red pandas. Its own visitor information makes one thing clear: this is popular enough that timed reservations and crowd control are a real concern.

Then there is the other Chengdu problem: mapo tofu, dan dan noodles, hotpot, rabbit-head discourse, tea-house afternoons and the general possibility of planning the entire trip around eating.

## what I would actually do

- Panda base early in the morning.
- Spend an afternoon in a tea house and refuse to optimize it.
- One serious Sichuan hotpot night.
- Dujiangyan / Qingchengshan day or overnight.
- Leave time for wandering neighbourhood food rather than stacking attractions.
