# Hulunbuir, Inner Mongolia

![Hulunbuir grasslands](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Hulunbuir%20Grasslands%2C%20Inner%20Mongolia%20-%209758734754.jpg?width=1200)

## why this is here

Because **Nantong → Hailar is currently about 3h35 nonstop**.

That is a deeply funny amount of flight time for the geographic transformation involved: leave humid lower-Yangtze Jiangsu around lunch, land in Hulunbuir with grassland, enormous sky, horses, wetlands, border-country weirdness and road-trip scale.

This is exactly the kind of route the Nantong hub exists to exploit.

## the wait-what moment

Current September 2026 schedule data shows about **six nonstop departures a week** on NTG → HLD, with Air Travel operating four of them in the active schedule.

Hailar itself is the airport/city gateway. The actual trip is the landscape outside it: the Morigele River and grasslands, Erguna wetlands, Hulun Lake, Heishantou, and — if the loop gets ambitious — Manzhouli on the Russian border.

The proposition is less “weekend in Hailar” and more **“three and a half hours on a plane buys access to one of China's great horizon trips.”**

## what it seems like

A road trip where distance is part of the pleasure.

Green steppe in summer; golden grass and sharper light in early autumn; serious winter if somebody decides to become difficult on purpose. The landscape wants long drives, horse rides, river bends, roadside food and enough time to stop because the view suddenly got ridiculous.

## things worth doing

- Follow the Morigele River through the grassland rather than treating one scenic gate as the entire experience.
- Spend time around Erguna / Heishantou for wetlands and open country.
- Eat lamb. This is mandatory research.
- Build a longer loop toward Hulun Lake and Manzhouli if there are 4–5 days available.
- Stay somewhere that gets properly dark at night.

## getting there

**Checked: 2026-09-04**

- NTG → HLD nonstop: about **3h35** in current schedules.
- Current route data: about **6 departures/week** in the active schedule.
- Route is seasonal enough that exact travel dates deserve a fresh check.

## rough trip idea

**4 days:** fly to Hailar, grassland/Morigele, Erguna/Heishantou, then back.

**5–6 days:** extend west toward Hulun Lake and Manzhouli and make it a proper loop.

This feels especially strong as a Jiangsu summer escape: choose the week when the lower Yangtze is soupy and go stare at the horizon instead.
