# research queue

Places that have passed the first test — **wait, what the hell is over there?** — but still need a proper page.

This is intentionally a mix of obvious places approached from a less-obvious angle and genuinely obscure logistics rabbit holes.

## from Shanghai / lower Yangtze

### rail-first

- **Suzhou** — stop treating it as “near Shanghai” and actually do gardens + canals properly.
- **Nanjing** — ~1h on the quickest HSR; city wall, museums, republican history, Purple Mountain.
- **Shaoxing** — canals + yellow wine + Lu Xun + old Zhejiang.
- **Wuzhen / Nanxun** — find the version of water-town travel that feels least theme-parked.
- **Wuyuan** — old Hui villages / spring rapeseed / link to Shangrao.
- **Sanqingshan** — granite mountain landscape accessed through Jiangxi rail network.
- **Wenzhou + Yandangshan** — southern Zhejiang cliffs / coast / mountain.
- **Ningbo + Zhoushan islands** — go beyond Putuoshan and map the archipelago.
- **Moganshan / Anji** — bamboo / mountain retreats / family weekend without a flight.

### short international hops

- **Busan** — seafood + coast + Korean city without defaulting to Seoul.
- **Taipei + Beitou** — city food + hot springs + mountains.
- **Kaohsiung / Tainan** — slower southern Taiwan trip.
- **Nagasaki** — Kyushu history / harbour city; determine best Shanghai routing.
- **Kagoshima** — volcano city; see if current PVG nonstop survives seasonally.
- **Sapporo / Hokkaido** — winter contrast trip.
- **Hanoi / Ninh Binh** — short flight then karst-on-land extension.

## from Nantong

- **Hailar / Hulunbuir** — seasonal grassland escape; check exact summer schedule.

## rule for promoting something to a full page

A candidate graduates when at least one of these becomes true:

1. The travel time is offensively short for how different the place feels.
2. The fare is offensively low.
3. The route solves an annoying ground journey.
4. The destination contains something we genuinely did not know existed.
5. Somebody is standing in an airport, sees the name on a board, and says **what the hell is that?**
