# from Shanghai

Shanghai is where this repo stops being a Frankfurt layover joke and becomes genuinely useful.

PVG is an absurd launchpad. A place can feel like a full international trip on the map and still be **90 minutes to 3 hours away by air**.

Route snapshot checked **2026-08-29**.

## immediate danger zone

| place | direct from Shanghai? | flight-ish | why we care |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| [Jeju, South Korea](../places/jeju-south-korea.md) | yes, PVG → CJU | ~1h35 | volcanic island, Hallasan, lava tubes, sea |
| [Fukuoka, Japan](../places/fukuoka-japan.md) | yes, PVG → FUK | ~2h05 | yatai, ramen, compact city, easy food weekend |
| [Okinawa, Japan](../places/okinawa-japan.md) | yes, PVG → OKA | ~2h40 | Kerama reefs, subtropical islands, beach mode |

### Jeju

PVG → CJU is currently around **1h35 nonstop**, with multiple carriers on the route. That is ridiculous. Jeju is closer in flight time than plenty of domestic trips inside China.

### Fukuoka

PVG → FUK is around **2h05 nonstop** and the current schedule shows roughly **three flights a day**. This may be the strongest pure “eat everything for three days” option.

### Okinawa

PVG → OKA is around **2h40 nonstop**. Current schedules show China Eastern and Spring Airlines operating the route.

## current verdict

Shanghai gives the family access to **East Asia as casual weekend geography**.

The key mental update is simple: stop asking whether a place feels “internationally far.” Look at the actual travel time.
