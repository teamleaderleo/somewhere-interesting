# from Shanghai

Shanghai is the launchpad where the map starts lying to you.

Places that *feel* like trips can be **30–90 minutes away by high-speed rail**, while Jeju / Kyushu / Okinawa sit in the same rough time budget as many domestic Chinese flights.

Route snapshot checked **2026-08-29**.

## ring 0: barely counts as travel

| place | how | time-ish | why bother |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Suzhou | HSR | ~25–40m on quick trains | gardens, canals, silk-city old Jiangnan |
| Wuxi | HSR | ~25–55m | Taihu, old canals, food, easy day trip |
| [Hangzhou](../places/hangzhou-china.md) | HSR | ~32m–1h | West Lake, tea hills, overnight reset |
| Nanjing | HSR | ~59m+ | imperial/republican history, city wall, museums |

Shanghai ↔ Hangzhou and Shanghai ↔ Nanjing currently have **hundreds of high-speed services** in a day. This is the key mental update: these cities belong in the same spontaneous-decision category as crossing a metro area.

## ring 1: proper weekend, minimal suffering

| place | how | time-ish | why it gets interesting |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| [Qiandao Lake](../places/qiandao-lake-china.md) | direct HSR + road | quickest rail ~1h48 | forested reservoir / lake reset |
| Shaoxing | HSR | roughly 1–2h depending train | canals, yellow wine, Lu Xun, old Zhejiang |
| Ningbo | HSR | roughly 2h-ish | port city + gateway toward Zhoushan |
| Wuzhen / Nanxun | rail + road | variable | classic Jiangnan water-town weekend |
| Wenzhou | HSR | ~2h45–4h | gateway to southern Zhejiang coast / mountains |

## ring 2: this should feel farther than it does

### [Huangshan, Anhui](../places/huangshan-china.md)

Current Shanghai → Huangshan North examples cluster around **2h50–3h10**. Granite peaks, ancient pines, clouds, stone stairs, Chinese-landscape-painting source code.

### [Putuoshan, Zhejiang](../places/putuoshan-china.md)

Sacred Buddhist mountain **on an island**. Typical approach: HSR toward Ningbo, transfer to Zhujiajian wharf, then ferry. More friction than Hangzhou; much stronger threshold feeling.

### Wuyuan / Shangrao orbit

This is a future deep dive: old Hui-style villages, terraced country, and easy onward access toward Sanqingshan / Jiangxi once the exact train schedule makes sense.

Exact international schedules move around, so individual trip planning should always re-check the date.

## family-trip logic

**Day-trip brain:** Suzhou / Wuxi / Hangzhou.

**One-night brain:** Hangzhou, Nanjing, Shaoxing, water towns.

**2–3 night brain:** Huangshan, Putuoshan, Qiandao Lake, Jeju, Fukuoka.

**"we have five days, let's make it count" brain:** Okinawa island-hopping, deeper Kyushu, Wuyuan + Huangshan, or a Taiwan loop.

## current verdict

Shanghai does two different things at once:

1. **Jiangnan becomes local geography by rail.**
2. **East Asia becomes casual weekend geography by air.**

We should exploit both.
