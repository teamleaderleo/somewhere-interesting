# somewhere interesting

A public notebook for places that make us stop and go:

> wait, what the hell is over there?

This is less a travel guide and more an accumulating case for going places.

Some entries begin with deliberate research. Others begin because a departure board says **Hurghada**, an entire gate suddenly fills with German vacationers, and ten minutes later we discover that Frankfurt has a cheap direct portal to the Red Sea.

The useful version of the project now has four home launchpads too: **Shanghai, Nantong, Vancouver, and Toronto**.

## launchpads we actually care about

### [Shanghai](hubs/shanghai.md)

East Asia collapses inward from PVG. Current examples: **Jeju ~1h35, Fukuoka ~2h05, Okinawa ~2h40** nonstop.

### [Nantong](hubs/nantong.md)

The sleeper hub. Current examples: **Xiamen ~1h50, Zhangjiajie ~2h10, Chongqing ~2h35, Kunming ~2h50** nonstop.

### [Vancouver](hubs/vancouver.md)

The coast-and-North launcher. **Tofino, Haida Gwaii, Whitehorse and Yellowknife** all become much more plausible when you look at actual air links instead of the map.

### [Toronto](hubs/toronto.md)

The range launcher. **Mexico City ~4h40, Iceland ~5h35, São Miguel ~5h45, Guyana ~6h15** nonstop — plus a whole second rabbit hole where northern Ontario itself starts getting extremely empty.

## what belongs here

- places discovered by accident
- places that are genuinely reachable from where family already spends time
- strange or beautiful connections between places
- routes that make somewhere feel unexpectedly close
- photos, maps, prices, itineraries, and rabbit holes
- unreasonably enthusiastic notes
- useful facts with links back to where we found them

Prices and schedules are snapshots. Each entry should say when they were checked, because travel prices enjoy lying through time.

Images are credited on their destination pages. We prefer openly licensed / attributable images so this can remain a happy public repo instead of a copyright crime scene.

## adding somewhere

Start with [`place-template.md`](place-template.md), or ignore it completely if the place demands a rant first.

PRs from people who stumbled in are welcome. The admission test is simple: **make us want to look at the map.**
